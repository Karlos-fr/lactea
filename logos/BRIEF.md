# Brief logo — Lactea

Use your image generation tool to create **6 distinct logo concepts** for an app called **Lactea**,
and save each one as a PNG file in the current directory.

## The app
Lactea is an Android TV / Google TV music visualizer in the spirit of the classic MilkDrop plugin
from the early 2000s: psychedelic, fluid, hypnotic visuals (swirls, feedback trails, glowing
waveforms, kaleidoscopes) that react in real time to whatever music is playing on the TV.

The name comes from Latin *lactea* — "milky", as in *Via Lactea*, the Milky Way. It is a nod to
MilkDrop (milk) and to the cosmic look of the visuals (galaxy, nebula).

## Constraints
- App icon: square, readable at small size (48 px) and on a TV home screen seen from a sofa.
- Dark background friendly (TV launchers are dark).
- No text in the square icon concepts (except concept 6 which is a wordmark).
- Must NOT use or imitate the Winamp, MilkDrop, Spotify or Deezer logos, nor any existing brand.
- Simple, bold shapes; vivid but not garish colours; glow is welcome.

## Concepts to explore (one image each)
1. `lactea-1-milkdrop-galaxy.png` — a single milk drop whose splash crown turns into a spiral galaxy.
2. `lactea-2-wave-spiral.png` — a glowing audio waveform curling into a spiral / vortex.
3. `lactea-3-nebula-l.png` — a stylised letter "L" made of luminous nebula / plasma trails.
4. `lactea-4-kaleido-eye.png` — a circular kaleidoscopic mandala, like a hypnotic iris, in milky
   whites with cyan/magenta/violet accents.
5. `lactea-5-minimal-orb.png` — minimal flat geometric mark: an orb with concentric sound rings
   and a milky gradient, very clean and modern.
6. `lactea-6-wordmark-banner.png` — Android TV launcher banner, 16:9 landscape (320×180 ratio):
   the word "Lactea" in a custom rounded, fluid typeface with a small symbol from one of the
   concepts above, on a deep space-blue background.

Square concepts: 1024×1024. When done, list the files you created with a one-line description each.
