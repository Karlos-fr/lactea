# Brief logo v2 — Lactea

Use your image generation tool. Two reference images are attached:
- Image 1 (`lactea-1-milkdrop-galaxy.png`): the chosen **shape** — a milk drop falling into a splash
  crown that swirls into a spiral galaxy.
- Image 3 (`lactea-3-nebula-l.png`): the chosen **colouring** — the whole shape is filled with luminous
  nebula / plasma: violet, magenta, pink, cyan, electric blue, with bright white-hot filaments.

Goal: keep the composition of image 1, but colour it **entirely** the way image 3 is coloured. No part of
the symbol should stay plain milky white: the drop, the splash crown, the droplets and the galaxy spiral
are all made of glowing multicoloured nebula, on a deep navy / space background.

Constraints:
- App icon, square 1024×1024, no text.
- Must stay readable at 48 px and on a TV home screen: bold silhouette, clear contrast with the background.
- Do not imitate any existing brand (Winamp, MilkDrop, Spotify, Deezer...).

Create **4 variants** and save them in the current directory:
1. `lactea-v2-a.png` — faithful: image 1's exact composition, fully nebula-coloured like image 3.
2. `lactea-v2-b.png` — same idea, colours flowing along the spiral (cyan at the outer arm → violet →
   magenta → pink at the drop), more saturated.
3. `lactea-v2-c.png` — simplified silhouette (fewer droplets, thicker forms) for small sizes, same colouring.
4. `lactea-v2-banner.png` — Android TV launcher banner, 16:9 landscape (1280×720): variant (a) symbol on
   the left + the word "Lactea" in a rounded, fluid typeface whose letters are also filled with the same
   nebula colours, deep space background.

When done, list the files with a one-line description each.
